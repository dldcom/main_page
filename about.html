BUILT BY AN EDUCATOR

수업을 생각하는 사람이
직접 만드는 도구.

한국의 초등교사가 웹 기반 학습 게임과 시뮬레이션을 개발합니다. 도형의 움직임부터 자연 현상과 어휘까지, 학생이 개념을 직접 다루며 배울 수 있는 경험을 만듭니다.

2026년 5월 개발을 시작했으며, 포털 내 수업자료를 교사 커뮤니티 인디스쿨에 공유해 교사들이 교육자료로 활용했습니다.

ABOUT DLDCOM

Classroom ideas.
Thoughtful digital tools.

DLDCOM is an independent educational software project developed by an elementary school teacher in South Korea. Started in May 2026, it builds browser-based learning games and simulations, organized by grade and subject in a public portal.

Teaching materials available through the portal have been shared on Indischool, a Korean teacher community, and used by teachers. Public projects include games exploring transformations of shapes, river erosion and deposition, and Korean vocabulary.

We are exploring Claude API integration for drafting learning questions and teaching materials, with teachers reviewing and editing the output before use. This is a planned direction, not a feature currently offered by the portal.
